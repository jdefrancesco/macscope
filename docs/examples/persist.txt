Example:

  macscope persist

Purpose:

  Parse launchd LaunchAgent and LaunchDaemon property lists and explain suspicious persistence signals.

Default directories:

  /Library/LaunchAgents
  /Library/LaunchDaemons
  ~/Library/LaunchAgents

Targeted directory:

  macscope persist --dir testdata/launchd

JSON:

  macscope persist --json

Output sections:

  Launchd Persistence
    Directory, parsed-job, and finding counts.

  Findings
    Evidence-backed categories such as USER_WRITABLE_PERSISTENCE, SHELL_LAUNCHD_JOB, NETWORK_DOWNLOADER_PERSISTENCE, RUN_AT_LOAD, or KEEPALIVE_ENABLED.

  Jobs
    Parsed labels and effective programs.

Notes:

  This command is read-only. It does not unload, delete, quarantine, or modify launchd jobs.
