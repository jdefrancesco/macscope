Example:

  macscope vpn
  macscope vpn "Work VPN"
  macscope vpn --json --last 60m "Work VPN"

Purpose:

  Inspect VPN service state, utun interfaces, DNS/proxy/routes, recent VPN logs, and sleep/wake correlation.

Native tools:

  scutil, ifconfig, route, netstat, log, pmset

Notes:

  This command is read-only. It reports likely causes and evidence; it does not change network settings.
