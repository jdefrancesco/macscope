Expected shape:

  macscope completion <bash|zsh|fish>

Examples:

  macscope completion bash
  macscope completion zsh
  macscope completion fish

Quick local sourcing:

  source <(macscope completion bash)
  source <(macscope completion zsh)
  macscope completion fish | source

Runtime inspection flags are included:

  proc: --maps, --tree, --full, --json
  ipc: --watch, --interval, --count, --jsonl, --full, --json
  collect: --pid, --out, --reports, --json
