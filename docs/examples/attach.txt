Example:

  macscope attach 1234

Purpose:

  Explain likely LLDB attach failures from process identity, developer group membership, signing state, and recent policy logs.

Native tools:

  ps, dseditgroup, codesign, log

JSON:

  macscope attach --json --last 30m 1234

Notes:

  This command does not bypass macOS policy. It reports evidence such as taskgated, task_for_pid, tccd, amfid, or syspolicyd denial signals.
